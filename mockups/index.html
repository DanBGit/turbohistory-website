Design directions

Each is a fully committed direction across the same three templates — homepage, book page (Napoleon), and a collection hub (Conquerors, a template the live site does not yet have). Pick one. They are not meant to be blended. Hero imagery is an art-directed CSS stand-in; the generation prompts are in each direction's notes.

01b · Cinematic Archive — new fonts + sand timer

Same direction, two changes to compare against 01: Georgia and Futura replaced with self-hosted Source Serif 4 and Archivo (both free, both licensed for commercial use), and the logo's hourglass built as a running sand timer — large in the hero, small beside the reading-time lines. Start here.

01 · Cinematic Archive

Prestige documentary title sequence. Near-black ground, one monumental lit scene per page, display serif at film scale, gold rationed to the accent word and the CTA, permanent grain. Motion is atmospheric: minute-long drifts, one authored load moment.

02 · Pulp Poster

Paperback rack meets modern poster. Huge condensed all-caps, covers pinned at angles with hard offset shadows and no blur, running text on parchment panels, gold starbursts and rubber stamps. Motion is physical: things drop and land rather than fade.

HomeBookCollectionNotes

03 · Museum Minimal

A national museum that decided wall text should be readable. Parchment ground, giant serif with a ghosted first line, covers matted and framed as exhibits, tiny uppercase labels, gold used only where a museum uses brass.
